At rest it is a brushed-black pill with a small lime tab on its left edge. On hover (or keyboard focus) the tab widens across the whole button in 200ms, revealing five double chevrons drawn as dots.

Each chevron is ten SVG circles. They share one keyframe that brightens and grows a dot, then settles it back, and every dot starts a little later than the last, 50ms down its own chevron and 120ms per chevron along the row, so the wave rolls left to right.

The tab colours and the dot colour are props, so the same button can carry a different accent without touching its motion. In the dark theme the metal inverts to a pale finish.
