> What do you do when the tool that got you here becomes the thing holding you back?

Transforming a marketing website into a scalable product platform — designed and built end to end.

**Role** Product Designer & Front-end Developer · **Duration** 2025–Present (in staging)

## Context

As Mahaana grew from a single investment product into a wealth-management platform, the website struggled to keep pace. It was originally built in Webflow, which had carried the company's early growth well. But as new products, campaigns, and content needs piled up, the platform got harder and harder to maintain.

Rather than ship yet another visual refresh, I proposed rebuilding the site from the ground up — a faster, more scalable platform that served both our customers and our internal teams.

## The Problem

The website wasn't failing because of its design. It was failing because of its workflow and technical limits.

Marketing and Operations leaned on me for even the smallest content updates — Webflow's interface felt too complex to touch, so routine changes that should have taken minutes became design requests that slowed the whole team down.

Meanwhile, our fund pages already showed live NAVs and real-time performance, but doing that in Webflow meant piling on custom code and JavaScript workarounds. As features grew, those scripts got brittle and started dragging down performance.

- Marketing and Operations couldn't confidently manage content on their own.
- Live fund data depended on custom scripts and code embeds inside Webflow.
- Growing customization hurt both maintainability and performance.
- The architecture wasn't flexible enough for Mahaana's expanding product line.

## What I Built

Instead of extending Webflow further, I redesigned and rebuilt the entire site on a modern, component-based architecture. This wasn't just a redesign — it changed how the website was built, managed, and maintained.

- **Next.js and React** — a scalable front-end foundation.
- **Tailwind CSS and reusable components** — faster development, consistent UI.
- **Sanity CMS** — full content ownership for Marketing and Operations.
- **Framer Motion** — smoother, more engaging interactions.
- **Direct API integrations** — live fund data without the Webflow workarounds.
- **Cursor** — an AI-assisted workflow to move faster while keeping production-quality code.

## Impact

The rebuild improved both the customer experience and internal operations.

- **Customers** — a modern, product-first experience with faster loads, smoother interactions, better accessibility, and real-time fund information through a cleaner architecture.
- **Internal teams** — Marketing and Operations now manage content independently in Sanity, no longer waiting on Design or Engineering for routine updates. Reusable components make launching new pages and campaigns far quicker.

## Lessons

This project taught me that great product design reaches well beyond beautiful interfaces. The biggest problem here wasn't visual — it was operational.

By rebuilding from the ground up, I wasn't just improving the customer experience; I was removing friction for the people maintaining the site every day. Owning both the design and the implementation showed me how architecture, performance, accessibility, and internal workflows all feed into a successful product.

The most valuable outcome wasn't a perfect Lighthouse score. It was a platform that lets teams move faster — and a solid foundation for Mahaana's next stage of growth.
