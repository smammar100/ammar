## Pakistan saves. It just doesn't invest.

The licence was real and the funds were already trading; what was missing was everything between a licence and a customer. It was a trust problem, not an investing one: Pakistanis already save, in gold at home and idle bank accounts, but moving that into an app meant trusting unfamiliar products, language and decisions with no branch to walk into. The work was to make it seamless enough that nobody would need one.

## The Design System

The design system is what made Mahaana work at scale. Four platforms and a roadmap of products not yet built all pulled on the same interface, so it needed one set of foundations, components and patterns rather than a fresh take per product. Every colour is a token with a light and a dark value, so one change lands on every product, platform and theme at once.

<MahaanaFoundations />

Gold and trading didn't exist yet when this was built. The product-tile pattern came first, so new products arrived into a shape already decided instead of arguing for their own. It also cut design-to-development handoff by 35%, by ending the back-and-forth over what a spacing value or a state should be.

## Five minutes, and nothing skipped

The goal was to make onboarding easier and faster. Before redesigning anything, I ran usability tests on the staging app and mapped how each stage felt.

<MahaanaJourneyMap />

Onboarding averaged a UX score of 78. The look was fine; the complaint was length, made worse by bugs. The regulated steps couldn't go, so the question became how to make each one feel worth finishing.

<MahaanaOnboarding />

Some steps now explain before they ask: the first screen sets Save+ against a bank account and Retirement against the income tax it saves. Others ask for less: one risk question per screen, the ID card photographed instead of typed, and answers turned straight into an allocation people can see and change.

The five-minute target was to invested, not submitted, so the first investment is chosen inside onboarding. An account that never gets funded is still a drop-off, just harder to see.

## Everything they own, on one screen

Once the money was in, the question became how it was going, so the home screen answers in the order people ask. How much do I have: total value and return, over a chart from one month to all time. Where is it: one card per product, with its value, return and risk level.

Below that sit the day's market data, a Wealth Advisor one tap away, and short reads for people new to investing. Someone who only came to check gets their answer before they scroll; the rest is there when they want more.

<MahaanaHomeAnnotated />

## Making Pakistan financially literate

Only 26% of adults in Pakistan are financially literate, and nobody invests in what they can't explain. So a core part of Mahaana's vision was to make investing easy for an ordinary saver to follow.

<MahaanaDawnArticle />

That became Discover, one tab from home: a snapshot of the Pakistani market, an AI chatbot that answers questions in plain language, videos and articles from Mahaana's YouTube channel, and the latest news. The aim was never to turn savers into analysts, just to give them enough of the language to trust the number on their screen.

<MahaanaDiscover />

## The whole product

Put together, one app covers saving, investing, retirement, gold, trading and insurance, and every part reads as one product, whichever door someone came in through.

## Reflection

**What worked.** The design system is what let the work survive four platforms. Simplifying a pension once, in a design file, is easy. Making it hold its meaning in every component, built by someone else months later, is the hard part.

**What I'd do differently.** Start the compliance conversation earlier. We treated the Shariah ruling as something to explain once the product existed; it would have been better as an input from the first sketch.

**What I'm taking forward.** In finance, trust has to be designed for, not claimed. Every claim on screen should be one the licence, the custodian and the compliance ruling can back.
